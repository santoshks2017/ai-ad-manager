# Stage 1: Problem Statement & MVP
## AI Ad Manager — Indian Auto Dealership Market

---

## Problem Statement
Indian car dealerships are bleeding ad budgets through agencies and in-house teams who lack the expertise and tooling to run Google and Meta campaigns efficiently — resulting in poor ROI, zero visibility into performance, and no control for the Dealer Principal.

---

## Target User Persona
**Primary: Dealer Principal (Dealership Owner)**
- Age: 35–60
- Owns 1–5 dealerships (single or multi-brand)
- Spends ₹50K–₹5L/month on digital advertising
- Relies 100% on agency or 1 in-house "digital person"
- Has no visibility into whether ads are actually working
- Pain: "I'm paying lakhs and don't know what I'm getting"
- Frustration: Agencies change constantly, no consistency, no accountability

**Secondary: Marketing Manager at dealership**
- Executes the campaigns but lacks professional tooling
- Currently juggling multiple browser tabs across Google Ads + Meta Ads Manager
- No single view of performance

---

## Current Alternatives & Why They Fail

| Alternative | Why It Fails |
|-------------|-------------|
| Local digital agencies | Generic approach, not auto-specific, high churn, hidden markups |
| In-house resource | Lacks expertise, uses basic tools, no benchmarks |
| Google Ads directly | Complex UI, no auto-specific templates, requires expertise |
| Meta Ads Manager | Separate from Google, no unified view, steep learning curve |
| Generic ad platforms (e.g. Madgicx) | Not India-specific, not auto-specific, no OEM/model targeting |

---

## Why Now
- India's auto industry sold 4.2M passenger vehicles in FY24 — growing 8% YoY
- Digital ad spend in auto sector growing at 35% YoY (Google India data)
- Dealers are under OEM pressure to generate enquiries digitally
- Meta and Google have both launched vertical ad formats for auto — but dealers don't know how to use them
- No India-specific, dealer-first ad management platform exists

---

## Core Use Case
A Dealer Principal logs in, connects their Google Ads and Meta Ads accounts, sees a single performance dashboard, and can launch a pre-built campaign for a specific car model in under 10 minutes — without needing an agency.

---

## Must-Have Features (MVP — Max 5)

1. **Unified Dashboard** — Single view of Google Ads + Meta Ads performance: spend, leads, CPL, impressions
2. **Campaign Launcher** — Pre-built campaign templates for car model launches, test drives, festive offers — dealer fills in model name, budget, duration and it goes live
3. **Lead Capture & Tracking** — Auto-capture leads from both Google and Meta into one inbox with source tagging
4. **Budget Manager** — Set monthly budgets per platform, get alerts when nearing limit, pause campaigns automatically
5. **Performance Reports** — Weekly auto-generated report (PDF/WhatsApp) for Dealer Principal showing what's working

---

## Nice-to-Have Features (Max 5)

1. AI-generated ad copy based on car model + offer type
2. Competitor ad intelligence (what nearby dealers are running)
3. OEM co-op fund tracker (dealers get reimbursement from manufacturers)
4. WhatsApp lead notification integration
5. Multi-dealership view for dealer groups

---

## Key Assumptions
- Dealers are willing to pay ₹5K–₹20K/month for a platform that replaces agency cost
- Google and Meta APIs are accessible for third-party ad management tools
- Dealer Principals will trust automation if they can see results clearly
- Existing agency relationships are weak enough to disrupt

---

## Risks
- Google/Meta API approval process can be slow (Partner program required)
- Dealers may resist giving ad account access to a new platform
- Agency lobby may push back or undercut pricing
- Low digital literacy among older Dealer Principals requires heavy onboarding support
