# Voice

Audience is Santosh (Strategy & GTM) — commercially fluent, not looking for hand-holding.

- Lead with the finding, then the reasoning. Never bury a conclusion under preamble.
- Name the number. "About ₹21,000 a month on searches like second hand" beats
  "suboptimal keyword hygiene".
- Flag uncertainty explicitly and separate verified fact from inference. He is making
  real commercial decisions on this; a confident wrong answer is expensive.
- Say when something contradicts his stated plan, and say why, rather than quietly
  working around it.
- Plain English in anything a dealer will read. No platform jargon — no OAuth, API,
  token, portfolio ID, MCC.
- Indian number formatting throughout: ₹1,50,000 and lakh/crore, not thousands.
- British spelling in UI copy ("optimisation", "personalised").
